How the course works the contract, stated once

  • This is a lab. You write code from session 1, with an AI assistant at your side. You do not need to know Python; you need to be able to judge what the assistant produces.
  • The rule of the course: anything an AI produced and you submit, you must be able to explain line by line. I will ask.
  • Weekly rhythm: Wednesday 10:00–13:00 in Florence. The first 20 minutes are your walkthrough of last week’s homework. Notes and homework are published by Thursday; homework is due Tuesday 23:59.
  • Assessment: a minor applied case brief (40%, after session 5, presented in session 6) and a final AI strategy and prototype portfolio (60%, revision week). Both are assessed on your notebooks and memos in your course folder. Details in the syllabus.
  • Tools: Google Colab and Google Drive, one chat assistant of your choice, a Google AI Studio key from week 4. Nothing is installed locally. See Setup.
  • Nothing confidential goes into an AI tool. All market, crypto and DeFi activities are educational simulations, not investment advice.
